# E-test Probe Mark Topology-induced Failure（IPFA 2021）

- 来源：IEEE 17th International Symposium on the Physical and Failure Analysis of Integrated Circuits (IPFA) 2021
- DOI：10.1109/IPFA53173.2021.9617372
- 页面：https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9617372/
- 访问日期：2026-09-25

## 关键摘录（公开摘要）

> In this paper, we show evidence of a unique failure at wafer sort that is caused by inline E-test probing on scribe line structures. This happens in spite of a safe probe distance from the [active area].

（摘要原文截取自 IEEE Xplore 检索摘要；完整正文需 IEEE 订阅。含义：划片线/边缘结构上的 E-test 探痕可以成为晶圆分选阶段的失效根因，即使探针与有源区保持"安全距离"。）

## 关联：die edge / seal ring 的作用
