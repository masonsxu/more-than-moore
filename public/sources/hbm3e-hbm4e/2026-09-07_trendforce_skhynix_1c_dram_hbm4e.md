# 来源 5：TrendForce — SK 海力士 1c DRAM 将于 1Q27 成为主力制程，为 HBM4E 做准备

- URL: https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/09/07/news-sk-hynix-1c-dram-reportedly-to-overtake-1b-as-main-process-in-1q27-as-it-prepares-for-hbm4e/
- 发布日期: 2026-09-07（转述 Chosun Biz / Yonhap / The Bell）
- 抓取日期: 2026-09-18

---

- SK hynix is rapidly expanding production of its 6th-generation 10nm-class (1c) DRAM, set to become the core memory die for HBM4E; HBM4E mass production planned for 2027. In July [2026] the company said it had already provided HBM4E samples to customers.
- 1c DRAM share of SK hynix production: ~10% in 1Q26, 13% in 2Q26 (vs ~16% at Samsung and 19% at Micron); projected ~24% in 3Q26 and 34% in 4Q26 (surpassing Samsung's ~31%), reaching 35% in 1Q27 — overtaking 1b (~33%) for the first time to become SK hynix's main DRAM process.
- SK hynix 2Q26 earnings call: 1c DRAM shipments began ramping during the quarter; stronger bit growth expected in H2 as HBM4 volumes increase.
- Different HBM4 approaches: Samsung adopted 1c DRAM for HBM4 targeting ~11.7 Gbps; SK hynix prioritized mass-production stability with proven 1b DRAM and MR-MUF packaging.
- 1c shift benefits: more bits per wafer → cost competitiveness; meaningful only if mass-production yields improve and customer qualification schedules keep pace (industry official).
- 1d race (The Bell): Samsung aims to complete 1d development in September [2026] and begin mass-production preparation in December; SK hynix targeting development completion in December and mass-production preparation in June 2027 — about six months behind. Samsung could begin initial mass production around end-2027. Samsung plans to use 1d DRAM in HBM5E, expected around 2030.
