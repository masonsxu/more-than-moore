# 来源 6：DigitalToday — 三星/SK 海力士 HBM4E 竞赛，量产时间点成关键战场

- URL: https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/53615/memory-big-two-near-hbm4e-race-mass-production-timeline-becomes-key-battleground
- 发布日期: 2026-05（三星 GTC 2026 展出 HBM4E 及 HBM4E core die 晶圆照片）
- 抓取日期: 2026-09-18

---

- Samsung disclosed HBM4E schedule and specifications on April 30 [2026] during its Q1 conference call: HBM4E supporting 16 Gbps per pin and 4.0 TB/s bandwidth; first sample in Q2 [2026].
- SK hynix: samples in H2 [2026], mass production in 2027. Sample timing 1–2 quarters later than Samsung, but mass production timing may converge in 2027 due to customer qualification and yield stabilization.
- Both based on the 1c (10nm-class 6th-generation) process.
- Samsung strategy: highlight performance specs (at HBM4 stage it "drove an upward adjustment of HBM4 performance specifications by using the most advanced process based on 1nm technology"; performance edge translated into price premium). HBM4 mass-production shipments began February [2026], first in the industry; HBM4 revenue expected to exceed half of total HBM revenue from Q3 [2026]; "all prepared capacity is sold out."
- SK hynix strategy: manufacturing stability and base die optimization. Quote (answering Citigroup): "Base die optimisation and applying the 1c nanometre process, which has proven the industry's top-tier performance, are key. Based on 1c mass production capability that has already reached a mature stage, we will supply stable performance and volume on time in HBM4 and HBM4E as well."
- Base die: component at the very bottom of an HBM stack acting as memory controller; its optimisation determines the HBM's power efficiency and signal processing performance.
- SK hynix: "Over the next three years, demand requested by customers already far exceeds our supply capabilities."
- Capacity: SK hynix moved up Yongin Phase 1 fab start-up from May 2027 to February 2027. Samsung: "demand for 2027 is being booked in advance."
- Backend packaging capacity (not just DRAM wafer capacity) is the timing variable deciding post-2027 market share.
